August 2026

New Publication in Thorax

Our new review, Breathing Immunity into In Vitro Lung Models, discusses the development of immune-competent lung models and their potential to improve disease modelling and therapeutic discovery. The review highlights key challenges in integrating immune cells and emerging solutions using organ-on-chip technologies, tunable extracellular matrices, bioprinting, and engineered microvascular networks.
