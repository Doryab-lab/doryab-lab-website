CURRENT OPPORTUNITIES

Two Funded PhD Positions

We are currently looking for two motivated PhD candidates to join the Translational Organ Microsystems (TOM) Group at the University of Würzburg.

PhD Position 1: 3D Bioprinting

We are seeking a highly motivated PhD candidate to work on advanced 3D bioprinting and biofabrication of functional tissue constructs. The project will involve 3D bioprinting of cell-laden constructs, development and optimisation of bioinks and printing parameters, and integration of bioprinted tissues with microphysiological and perfusion systems.

Applicants should hold a Master's degree (or equivalent) in biomedical engineering, bioengineering, biomaterials, materials science, tissue engineering, biotechnology, or a related field. Experience with biofabrication, biomaterials/hydrogels, cell culture, CAD, or additive manufacturing is advantageous.

PhD Position 2: Lung-on-Chip

We are recruiting a PhD candidate to develop advanced human lung-on-chip models. The project will combine biofabrication, biomaterials, tissue engineering, organ-on-chip technologies, and immunology to investigate disease mechanisms and support translational research.

We welcome applicants with backgrounds in biomedical engineering, bioengineering, biology, biomaterials, tissue engineering, biotechnology, infection and immunity, or related disciplines. Experience with mammalian cell culture is particularly valuable; experience in organ-on-chip technologies, microfluidics, biofabrication, microscopy, molecular biology, or immunological techniques is advantageous.

Application deadline for both PhD positions: 30 September 2026.
Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis, and the positions may be filled earlier if suitable candidates are identified.

Master's & MD Students

Master's and MD students are also welcome to contact us regarding research projects, theses, or related opportunities. These opportunities are primarily intended for students currently studying in Germany, preferably in the Würzburg area.

Applications & Inquiries

For PhD applications, please send a single PDF including a short motivation letter (max. one page), CV, academic transcripts, and contact details of two academic referees. For Master's and MD projects, please send a short inquiry with your CV to Dr. Ali Doryab ali.doryab@uni-wuerzburg.de
